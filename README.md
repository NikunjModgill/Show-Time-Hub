## ✨ Overview

**Show Time Hub** is a mobile-first UI/UX design for a modern movie ticket booking app.  
Built with user experience at its core, the app provides a seamless journey from movie discovery to seat booking — all through clean, intuitive screens designed in **Figma**.

---


> 💡 *All screens are designed and prototyped in Figma.*

---

## 🎯 Features

- 🎞️ Browse Now Showing & Upcoming Movies
- 📅 Pick Date, Time & Theater
- 🎟️ Interactive Seat Selection
- 💳 Booking Summary & Confirmation
- 👤 Sign In / Sign Up Screens
- 🔥 Modern UI with cinematic dark theme

## 🧠 Design Process

- 🕵️‍♂️ Research on UX patterns from BookMyShow & Fandango
- ✏️ Wireframing to map out user flows
- 🎨 High-fidelity mockups with focus on mobile-first experience
- 🔁 Interactive prototyping in Figma

---

## 📸 UI Highlights

> "Great UI is invisible — it just feels right."

- Minimalistic, thumb-friendly navigation
- Color-coded seat selection for clarity
- Smooth and visually appealing transitions
- Focus on accessibility & readability
